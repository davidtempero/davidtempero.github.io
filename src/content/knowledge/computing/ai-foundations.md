## What this section brings together

The vocabulary and technical pieces needed to reason about modern AI systems: models, context windows, prompts, embeddings, retrieval, tool calls, and inference. It is a conceptual map rather than a catalogue of frameworks.

## Useful starting points

- [Google AI](https://ai.google/) — a broad entry point to models, research, APIs, and responsible-AI resources.
- [Repomix](https://github.com/yamadashy/repomix) — a practical example of context engineering: it packages a codebase into an AI-friendly representation.
- [llmfit](https://github.com/AlexsJones/llmfit) — helps match local models and providers to available hardware.

## Questions to ask of a design

When evaluating an AI feature, establish the task, the source of truth, the context budget, how outputs are checked, and what happens when the model is uncertain. Those decisions matter more than the choice of model alone.
